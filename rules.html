Server Rules

Follow these rules to keep BlurMc fair, friendly and enjoyable for everyone.

⚠️

Please read before playing

By playing on BlurMc, you agree to follow these rules. Staff members may take action against players who break them.

01
EVERYONE

General Rules

1.

Respect Everyone

Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against or intentionally annoy other players.

2.

No Spam

Do not flood chat with repeated messages, characters or commands.

3.

No Advertising

Advertising other Minecraft servers or communities without permission is not allowed.

4.

Follow Staff Instructions

Staff decisions must be respected. If you disagree with a punishment, use the appropriate appeal method.

5.

No Exploiting

Do not abuse bugs, glitches or unintended mechanics for an advantage.

02
SURVIVAL

Survival Rules

1.

No Griefing

Do not destroy or modify another player's builds without permission.

2.

No Stealing

Taking items from another player's protected or private area is prohibited.

3.

No Unfair Advantages

Hacks, cheats and unauthorized modifications that provide an unfair advantage are prohibited.

4.

Respect Builds

Do not intentionally block, surround or interfere with another player's build.

03
LIFESTEAL

Lifesteal Rules

1.

No Combat Logging

Leaving the server during active combat to avoid death is not allowed.

2.

No Boosting

Do not intentionally give kills, hearts or advantages to another player through arranged deaths.

3.

No Alt Abuse

Using alternate accounts to gain unfair hearts, items or advantages is prohibited.

4.

No Exploits

Duplication glitches, exploits and unintended mechanics must not be used.

STAFF ACTION

Breaking the rules has consequences.

Depending on the severity of an offense, punishments may include warnings, mutes, kicks, temporary bans or permanent bans.
